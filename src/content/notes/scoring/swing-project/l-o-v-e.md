## Overview

Eb for first vocal, F for last vocal.

Decided to avoid the classic transpose up directly, using a middle section in Bb. Bb is a common chord between start and end keys.

## Rhythm

Style with emphasis on the horns laying down the groove.

## Voicings

Closed voicings.

Use diatonic passing chords.

## Key Structure

Starting in Eb (3 flats), to Bb (2 flats), to F (1 flat).
