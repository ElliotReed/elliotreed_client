## Discography

### Songs for Swingin' Lovers!

    - 1956
    - arranger: Nelson Riddle

    This collaboration marked a significant moment in Sinatra's
    career, as Riddle's arrangements infused the album with a 
    vibrant, jazz-inflected swing that contrasted with the more 
    somber tone of their earlier work, In the Wee Small Hours.
    Riddle's innovative orchestration, characterized by its rhythmic
    vitality and sophisticated harmonies, played a crucial role in
    revitalizing classic standards and contributed to the album's
    enduring acclaim.
