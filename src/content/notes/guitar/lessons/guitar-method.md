## What to Expect
### Uncomfortableness
There is nothing natural about playing an instrument. Your hands are not meant to do the things you will ask them to do. However, you and your hands are very adaptable, and can learn almost anything. The first time you do something on your instrument your hands complain and it seems barley possible. The thousandth time you do it, it seems so natural that you can't imagine how you struggled before. It will always feel awkward, then it ***will*** feel natural.
